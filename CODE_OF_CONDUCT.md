# Code of Conduct

Dagwick follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, and
anywhere someone represents the project) and to everyone taking part,
maintainers included.

In short: be welcoming and kind, criticise ideas and code rather than people,
and accept that other people may disagree with you. Harassment, insults,
personal attacks and publishing anyone's private information are not
tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at
**TODO-conduct-contact**. Reports are read only by the maintainers. If a
report is about a maintainer, that maintainer is left out of handling it.

We will acknowledge a report within **3 working days** and keep the reporter's
identity confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide on a response using the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on how
serious the behaviour is and whether it is repeated.
